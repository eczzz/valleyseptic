A Journey of Service and Community

Our Story: Rooted in Family

Valley Septic & Plumbing’s story is one of family, dedication, and deep community roots. Our journey began with a single van and a vision to provide reliable plumbing services to our Fraser Valley neighbors.

From those humble beginnings, we’ve grown alongside our community. We expanded from basic plumbing to comprehensive septic services, meeting the evolving needs of our rural areas. As word spread of our reliable work and fair pricing, our family of skilled professionals grew, as did our fleet of fully-equipped vans.

Throughout our evolution, one thing has remained constant: our commitment to treating every customer like family. Whether it’s a leaky faucet or a complex septic system, each job is an opportunity to build lasting relationships.

Today, we proudly serve the entire Fraser Valley with the same values that got us started – integrity, hard work, and a deep love for our community. As we look to the future, we’re excited to continue growing with and serving the place we call home.
